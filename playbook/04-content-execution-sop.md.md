# 04 – Content Execution SOP

> **Purpose**
>
> This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) converts the Content Operating System (Document 03) into a repeatable execution process.
>
> The objective is to ensure that every LinkedIn content asset is planned, created, reviewed, distributed and measured using the same quality standards.
>
> This SOP is designed for B2B SaaS marketing teams but can also be executed by a single marketer or founder.

---

# SOP Overview

| Phase | Objective | Primary Owner |
|---------|-----------|---------------|
| Phase 1 | Collect customer insights | Marketing + Sales |
| Phase 2 | Plan content | Content Strategist |
| Phase 3 | Produce content | Content Marketer |
| Phase 4 | Review & approve | Editor / Marketing Lead |
| Phase 5 | Publish & distribute | Marketing Operations |
| Phase 6 | Measure & improve | Marketing Team |

Every phase must be completed before the next begins.

Skipping steps introduces quality risks that compound over time.

---

# SOP 01 — Customer Insight Collection

## Objective

Identify customer problems worth solving through content.

Content should never originate from assumptions or internal brainstorming alone.

The strongest ideas consistently emerge from customer conversations, sales objections and product feedback.

---

## Owner

Marketing Team

Supported by:

- Sales
- Customer Success
- Product Marketing
- Founder

---

## Inputs

- Sales call recordings
- Customer interviews
- Support tickets
- Product feedback
- Community discussions
- Industry news
- CRM notes

---

## Process

### Step 1

Collect customer questions from all available sources.

Do not edit or summarise them initially.

Capture customer language exactly as it appears.

---

### Step 2

Cluster similar problems into themes.

Examples:

- Pricing objections
- AI adoption
- Content strategy
- Product positioning
- Hiring
- ROI

AI may assist with clustering.

Human review is mandatory.

---

### Step 3

Prioritise themes using three criteria.

| Question | Yes / No |
|-----------|----------|
| Does this problem appear repeatedly? | □ |
| Does solving it help the target customer? | □ |
| Does it align with company positioning? | □ |

Only topics answering "Yes" to all three questions progress.

---

## Deliverables

- Customer insight repository
- Theme library
- Prioritised content backlog

---

## Quality Checklist

Before moving to the next phase:

- [ ] Customer language preserved
- [ ] Multiple sources reviewed
- [ ] Themes prioritised
- [ ] Duplicate ideas removed
- [ ] AI output manually reviewed

---

## Common Failure Modes

❌ Internal brainstorming without customer evidence

❌ Choosing topics based on trends alone

❌ Ignoring recurring customer objections

❌ Editing customer wording too early

---

## Why This Step Exists

Every practitioner consistently returned to customer understanding as the starting point for effective marketing.

Without customer insight, content becomes opinion-driven instead of customer-driven.

# SOP 02 — Content Planning

## Objective

Transform validated customer problems into a structured publishing roadmap.

Planning determines **what deserves to be created** before execution begins.

---

## Owner

Content Strategist

---

## Inputs

- Customer insight repository
- GTM priorities
- Product roadmap
- Seasonal initiatives
- Existing content library

---

## Process

### Step 1

Review the prioritised customer problems.

Remove topics already covered recently.

---

### Step 2

Convert problems into content opportunities.

Examples:

Problem

↓

Educational post

↓

Carousel

↓

Founder opinion

↓

Case study

↓

Newsletter

---

### Step 3

Score every idea.

| Criteria | Score (1–5) |
|-----------|-------------|
| Customer relevance | |
| Originality | |
| Business value | |
| Ease of execution | |
| Long-term usefulness | |

Only high-scoring ideas enter production.

---

## Deliverables

- Monthly content calendar
- Publishing schedule
- Content briefs
- Success metrics

---

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] Customer problem clearly defined
- [ ] Unique angle identified
- [ ] Content objective documented
- [ ] Distribution opportunity identified
- [ ] Success metric assigned

---

## Common Failure Modes

❌ Publishing because the calendar requires it

❌ Choosing topics competitors already dominate

❌ No original perspective

❌ Planning without GTM alignment

---

## Why This Step Exists

The strongest practitioners consistently create content intentionally.

They rarely publish simply to maintain consistency.

Planning determines strategic value before execution begins.
