# LinkedIn Organic Content Strategy for B2B SaaS

An evidence-informed **LinkedIn Organic Content Playbook / SOP** built from research across ten B2B SaaS practitioners.

The purpose of this repository is not simply to collect expert advice. It is to evaluate practitioner evidence, compare areas of agreement and disagreement, make explicit decisions, and translate those conclusions into an operating model that can be executed.

---

The **Execution Playbook / SOP is the primary deliverable** of this repository.

The supporting documents explain the research, operating principles, disagreements, rejected recommendations, original ideas, limitations, expert evaluation, sources and decision-making that informed the final Playbook.

# Evidence & Attribution Standard

A core principle of this repository is distinguishing between three different types of content.

### Practitioner Evidence

What a practitioner actually recommended, demonstrated or discussed in their published work.

### My Synthesis

Conclusions formed by comparing multiple practitioner sources and identifying patterns across them.

### Original Ideas

Ideas developed through my own reasoning during the research and Playbook development process that I could not trace directly to a specific practitioner source.

These categories are intentionally kept separate throughout the repository.

Where a recommendation is derived from practitioner evidence, the underlying source is documented.

Where a conclusion represents my interpretation, it is treated as synthesis rather than presented as an expert recommendation.

Where the evidence was insufficient to attribute a position confidently, I have identified that limitation rather than manufacturing an attribution.

# What This Repository Is — and Is Not

This repository is an **evidence-informed operating model**, not a universal formula for LinkedIn growth.

It does not assume that:

- one publishing cadence works for every organisation;
- one expert has the answer to every marketing problem;
- engagement automatically translates into business outcomes;
- AI-generated content is inherently better or worse than human-created content;
- or practitioner advice can be transferred without considering its original context.

The Playbook is designed to make those assumptions visible so they can be tested and revised.

---

# Final Operating Principle

The central principle behind the repository is:

> **Use research to inform decisions, use systems to make execution repeatable, and use human judgment to determine what should actually be published.**

The goal is not maximum content output.

The goal is to create a sustainable system for producing differentiated, useful and commercially relevant LinkedIn content while remaining explicit about what is known, what is inferred and what still needs to be tested.
